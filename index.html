About UNYAZI PATHWAYS

UNYAZI PATHWAYS specializes in Mental Health First Aid, emotional wellness support, suicide watch awareness, and community-centered care.

Like CPR provides immediate physical first aid, Mental Health First Aid provides immediate emotional support before referring individuals to professionals.

We believe emotional wellness support should be accessible, compassionate, culturally relatable, and community-centered.
